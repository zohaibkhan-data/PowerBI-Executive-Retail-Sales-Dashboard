# Executive Retail Sales Dashboard (Power BI)

## Project Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to track executive-level retail KPIs, regional profitability across Pakistan, sales channels, and payment methods.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Total Revenue:** $4.38M
- **Total Profit:** $112.63K
- **Profit Margin:** 2.57%

## Key Business Insights
- **Top Product Category:** Electronics generates the vast majority of revenue ($4.1M), followed by Home & Kitchen ($0.2M).
- **Regional Performance:** Khyber Pakhtunkhwa leads in total profit ($43.31K), closely followed by Punjab ($39.84K) and Sindh ($27.79K).
- **Sales Channels:** Revenue is well-balanced across In-Store (44.8%), Online (27.96%), and Distributor (27.36%) channels.

## Tools & Techniques Used
- **Power BI Desktop** (Data Visualization & Interactive Slicers)
- **Power Query** (Data Cleaning & Transformation)
- **DAX** (Calculated Measures for Revenue, Profit, and Profit Margin)
